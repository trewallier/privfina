# Prémium Magyar Állampapír (PMÁP)

This folder contains the first inflation-linked product specification in the government-security family.

The PMÁP specification models product-type behavior, not a complete live market feed or a full historical registry of PMÁP series.

Inflation lookup integration and series registry maintenance are future work.

Worked examples in this folder are executable acceptance scenarios for the product specification model; they are not authoritative market quotes.
